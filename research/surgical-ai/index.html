Surgical AI Paper Map

Venue type

Selected venues are top AI conferences (NeurIPS, ICLR, AAAI, JMLR and others), top CV conferences (CVPR, ICCV, WACV and others), robotics conferences and journals (ICRA, IROS, CoRL, RA-L), MICCAI and IPCAI with their workshops, and medical and surgical AI journals (MedIA, TMI, IJCARS, the Nature, Science and JAMA families, npj, Ann Surg, Br J Surg, Surg Endosc and others).

Years follow the conference or journal publication, and papers without a formal publication use their arXiv release year. The ~2017 column holds early papers from 2014 to 2017.

Surveyed as of October 2026. ‘Inferred’ after a group name means the affiliation was inferred from the author list, and cards without a group could not be matched to an affiliation.
