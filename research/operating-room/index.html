Operating Room AI Paper Map

Venue type

Selected venues are top AI conferences (NeurIPS, AAAI and others), top CV conferences (CVPR, WACV and others), robotics conferences (ICRA, IROS), MICCAI and IPCAI with their workshops, and medical and surgical AI journals (MedIA, TMI, IJCARS, the Nature and JAMA families, npj, Ann Surg, Surg Endosc and others).

Years follow the conference or journal publication, and papers without a formal publication use their arXiv release year. The ~2017 column holds early papers from 2008 to 2017.

Surveyed as of October 2026. ‘Inferred’ after a group name means the affiliation was inferred from the author list, and ‘Unconfirmed’ means the affiliation could not be confirmed.
